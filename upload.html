Choose your capture method

We process both photos and videos into immersive 3D. Pick the one that's easiest for you.

📸

Photos (Stitch)

Take 8-12 overlapping photos while turning on the spot. Best for interiors with good lighting. Works on any phone.

🎥

Video (3D Scan)

Record a slow walkthrough video covering every room. Produces a more immersive result. Recommended for larger spaces.

📸 How to take photos for 360°

📍 Stand in one spot Keep your feet planted. Turn your body, not your feet.
🔄 Overlap 30% Each photo should overlap the previous by about a third.
📐 8-12 photos per room Take a full circle. More photos = smoother result.
💡 Good lighting Turn on all lights. Open curtains. Avoid harsh shadows.
Hold phone vertically, keep it level
Use zoom, flash, or HDR mode
Move slowly and steadily
Photograph mirrors or glass directly

Pro tip: For best results, mount your phone on a small tripod or selfie stick held steady at chest height. One circle per room — take 8-12 shots.

🎥 How to record video for 3D

🚶 Walk slowly Slow walking pace. Each room takes 15-30 seconds.
🔄 Pan the camera Slowly look left and right as you walk to capture walls and corners.
🏠 Cover every room Don't skip rooms. Walk through doorways, show the layout.
📹 Keep it steady Hold phone with two hands. No sudden movements.
Record at 1080p 30fps or higher
Use fisheye or wide-angle lens
Show the entrance and each room entry
Film people, pets, or reflective surfaces

Pro tip: Start at the front door, walk through each room naturally, and end at the back. A single continuous video is ideal. Aim for 2-5 minutes total.
